**Rule for an 11-week preparation window:** select a topic and read only necessary sections, then immediately solve the corresponding official questions. Do not read each textbook cover to cover.

## Solving workflow

1. **Official syllabus:** check this topic is examinable.
2. **Concept:** derive in your own notebook from fundamentals; note assumptions and dimensions.
3. **Original daily ten:** complete one dated sheet at your target time, without looking at answers.
4. **PYQ archive:** solve the official questions covering the same concept.
5. **Error review:** record the root cause, not just the final answer.
6. **Mock:** transfer the skill to mixed questions under the clock.
